📖 Materi Bahasa Inggris

Kelas 3 SD • Baca dulu sebelum mengerjakan kuis ya!

🐶 1. Animals (Hewan)

Kita belajar nama-nama hewan dalam bahasa Inggris.

IndonesiaEnglish
KucingCat 🐱
AnjingDog 🐶
BurungBird 🐦
IkanFish 🐟
SapiCow 🐮
KelinciRabbit 🐰
📌 Contoh: I have a cat. (Aku punya kucing.)

🎨 2. Colors (Warna)

Warna-warna dasar dalam bahasa Inggris:

  • Red = Merah
  • Blue = Biru
  • Yellow = Kuning
  • Green = Hijau
  • Black = Hitam
  • White = Putih
📌 Contoh: The sun is yellow. (Matahari berwarna kuning.)

🔢 3. Numbers (Angka 1-20)

AngkaEnglishAngkaEnglish
1One11Eleven
2Two12Twelve
3Three13Thirteen
4Four14Fourteen
5Five15Fifteen
📌 Contoh: I have three apples. (Aku punya tiga apel.)

👨‍👩‍👧 4. Family (Keluarga)

  • Father = Ayah
  • Mother = Ibu
  • Brother = Saudara laki-laki
  • Sister = Saudara perempuan
  • Grandfather = Kakek
  • Grandmother = Nenek
📌 Contoh: My mother is kind. (Ibuku baik.)

📝 5. Simple Greetings (Sapaan)

  • Good morning = Selamat pagi
  • Good afternoon = Selamat siang
  • Good evening = Selamat malam (saat bertemu)
  • Good night = Selamat tidur
  • Thank you = Terima kasih
  • You're welcome = Sama-sama
📌 Contoh: Good morning, teacher! (Selamat pagi, Bu Guru!)

⭐ 6. To Be (am, is, are)

Kata am, is, are artinya "adalah".

SubjekTo BeContoh
IamI am a student.
He / She / ItisShe is my sister.
You / We / TheyareThey are happy.
📌 Ingat! I → am • He/She/It → is • You/We/They → are
